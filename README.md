**Last Lantern** — an original arena-defense game in vanilla JavaScript/Canvas. You are the Keeper, defending the Last Lantern at the center of a twilight meadow through 10 nights against the Gloam: moths that bite the flame out, Cinderback Beetles that latch on and drain it, and Sputter Imps that spit gloom from range. WASD/arrows to move, mouse to aim, hold to fire, Shift to dash. Between nights, choose 1 of 3 boons (from a pool of 9); gather kindling, hearts and frost pickups. Win at dawn after night 10 and unlock the endless "long dark" mode; lose when the flame or your hearts reach zero. Score is counted in Embers, with a persistent best. All art is drawn in code on canvas and all sound is procedural WebAudio — no external assets, no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8000/game/ in a desktop browser
# (or just open game/index.html directly)
```
